Frequently Asked Questions

Is the consultation fee adjustable against treatments?

Yes, if you book a treatment package on the same day as your consultation, the consultation fee is waived.

Are your laser treatments safe for all skin types?

Absolutely. We use US-FDA approved technologies that have specific modes designed to be completely safe and effective for all skin types.

Do I need to take time off work after a chemical peel?

It depends on the depth of the peel. We offer superficial 'lunchtime' peels with zero downtime, as well as deeper peels that may require a few days of recovery.
