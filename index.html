Our Expert

Meet Our Doctor

Board-certified dermatologist with extensive training in clinical dermatology, cosmetology, and hair restoration.

Dr. Sajin Alexander

MBBS, MD (Dermatology, Venereology & Leprosy), FRGUHS (Aesthetic Dermatology)

Director & Chief Dermatologist

Dr. Sajin Alexander is a board-certified Dermatologist with a fellowship in Aesthetic Dermatology from Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences (RGUHS), Karnataka, with extensive experience in Clinical Dermatology, Cosmetology and Hair Restoration procedures.

He is also trained in Hair Transplantation surgery and has more than 10 national and international publications in various journals and books. Having worked in close association with many renowned Dermatologists and institutes across the country, Dr. Sajin Alexander wants SkinClue to impart evidence-based knowledge and treatment options to patients for both aesthetic and clinical dermatology with utmost transparency.
